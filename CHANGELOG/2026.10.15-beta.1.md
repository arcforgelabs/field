## 2026.10.15-beta.1

### Highlights

- Quotes, invoices, payments and receipts are also kept as rows, and each is checked to the cent against what was issued. (#8, #11, #33)
- An invoice takes its number (INV-YYYY-M-N) when it is finalised, and an issued invoice is corrected by a credit note and a new invoice, never changed in place. (#39, #45, #47)
- One email can carry several issued documents, Send can be undone for 10 seconds, and the job lists every email as it went out. (#59, #64, #67, #69)
- Sentry reports errors, frustration and slow screens, and it never receives customer data, document numbers or money amounts. (#42, #62, #83)
- Owners connect Field to their own Xero app in Settings, and each issued invoice makes a matching draft in Xero with Field's PDF. (#94, #96, #107, #115)
- Field publishes verifiable releases to npm, GHCR and GitHub Releases, and an operator can run a pinned release that refuses to install unless it verifies. (#120, #121, #136)

### Changes

- The business profile comes from configuration, so a production installation can deploy from this repository. (#9)
- Every quote version is also rows, verified to the cent. (#8)
- Quote documents and totals come from the quote rows. (#11)
- Issued documents can also be kept in a locked R2 bucket. (#13)
- The business trading name is optional; documents use the legal name without one. (#23)
- Invoices, payments and receipts are also rows, verified against what was issued. (#33)
- Sentry reports every error, frustration and slow screen without customer data. (#42)
- Invoices are issued from the sequence (INV-YYYY-M-N) when they are finalised. (#39)
- An issued invoice is corrected by a credit note and a new invoice, never revised in place. (#45)
- Milestone invoices are tax invoices with one claim line per milestone; receipts take RCT-YYYY-M-N. (#47)
- One email carries several of the job's issued documents, by uid. (#59)
- The email composer follows Field's design system, with a preview built by the send. (#64)
- Send can be undone for 10 seconds, drafts are kept on the server, and the job lists every email as it went out. (#67)
- Replacement invoices attach their adjustment note by default, and follow-ups join the earlier Gmail conversation. (#69)
- Owners settle legacy invoice amounts on a screen, not the browser console. (#88)
- Owners connect Field to their own Xero app in Settings, Integrations. (#94)
- A Replit publish only builds a commit that passed GitHub CI, and main requires passing checks. (#111)
- Issued invoices create matching drafts in the owner's Xero, with Field's PDF. (#96)
- Field's invoice prints its job and quote, as its Xero invoice's reference does. (#107)
- Owners enter integration credentials in Settings, stored encrypted per organisation. (#115)
- A new installation has no business identity by default. (#123)
- Field publishes verifiable CalVer releases to npm, GHCR and GitHub Releases. (#120)
- Operators run a pinned Field release that refuses to install unless it verifies. (#121)
- FIELD_SECRETS_KEY can be a 40-character password from a password generator. (#126)
- Field's releases are named and channelled as openclaw/openclaw's are. (#136)
